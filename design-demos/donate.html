Santa's Knights, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

General information, not tax advice. Consult a tax professional about your situation.

  1. 01Cash donations are generally deductible up to 60% of your adjusted gross income (AGI).
  2. 02Property donations are typically deductible at 20–50% of value, depending on the type of property.
  3. 03Excess contributions above AGI limits can carry over for up to five years.
  4. 04Non-cash and vehicle donations are valued at fair market value for deduction purposes.
  5. 05Donors age 70½ and older may make qualified charitable distributions (QCDs) of up to $100,000 per year directly from IRAs.
