You never see the child. They never see you.

Names come off first

Before a letter ever reaches this page, we remove last names, addresses, and anything else that could point to a real child. A moderator checks every one.

Both sides stay private

Families don't see who adopted a wish, and donors don't see the family. Gifts are sent through Amazon, so no addresses change hands between you.

Keep it kind & safe

Send only what's age-appropriate, legal, and safe. Don't try to identify, contact, or locate a child or family. That's the whole deal.
