Asked often.

What is Santa's Letters?
Kids around Harlem write letters to Santa. We remove anything that could identify a child, then post the wishes so people can adopt them and send the gifts. It runs every December.
How do I adopt a letter?
Head to the Santa's Letters section to see how it works, or message us. You'll pick a wish, buy the gift, and send it. The child's identity stays private the whole way through.
Is everything really free?
Yes. We're a 501(c)(3), so the people we serve never pay. Donors and grants cover the classes, the gifts, and the events.
I want to train. Where do I start?
The classes, schedule, and booking live on our training site. Head there to find a class, or message us and we'll point you the right way.
