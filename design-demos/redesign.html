Giving and your taxes

We're tax-exempt federally and in New York State, and the receipt is emailed automatically.

IRS guidance ↗ Publication 526 ↗

General information, not tax advice. Confirm your situation with a tax professional.

How the deduction works

Donations come off your gross income. You have to itemize rather than take the standard deduction, so keep the receipt we send you.

Limits and carryover

Cash gifts are deductible up to 60% of adjusted gross income; property runs 20–50%. Anything above the limit carries forward five years.

Goods and vehicles

Non-cash donations are valued at fair market value. Special rules apply to vehicles.

Giving from an IRA at 70½+

Transfer up to $100,000 a year straight from an IRA. It can count toward your required minimum distribution and stays out of your taxable income.

Volunteering and estate gifts

You can't deduct your time, but travel and other volunteering costs are deductible. Bequests reduce a taxable estate.

Before you sign up

The questions we get asked most.

Anything else? contact@santasknights.org

Do I have to pay anything to train?

No. Class membership is $0 and always will be. Monthly giving is a separate, entirely optional thing — you never need it to walk in and train.

Do I need experience, or my own gear?

Neither. Beginners are the point. Foam weapons, armor, and mats are provided, and instructors run every floor.

Can I cancel monthly giving?

Any time, from the payment processor. There’s no minimum term and nothing to negotiate.

Is monthly giving tax-deductible?

Yes. Santa’s Knights, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3), tax-exempt federally and in New York State. The receipt is emailed automatically.

Where do classes actually happen?

The Manhattanville Community Center, 530 W 133rd St in Harlem — except Women’s Combat, which also runs from a Midtown studio. Schedule and booking live on gladiators.nyc.
